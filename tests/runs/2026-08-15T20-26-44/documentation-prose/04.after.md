## 2.4.0 — 2026-08-01

### Changed
- The search index updates one document at a time instead of rebuilding the whole index. Reindexing takes about 3 seconds instead of about 90 seconds.

### Fixed
- A document with no title opens without a crash. Opening it previously raised a null reference error.

### Removed
- The `--verbose` flag is removed. Use `--log-level debug` instead.

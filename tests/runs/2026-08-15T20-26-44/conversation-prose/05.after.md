## Image storage: R2 behind a Worker

R2 stores the uploaded images, and a Worker serves them. We rejected two alternatives: a public R2 bucket on a custom domain, and a third-party image CDN.

Private galleries require an authorisation check on every request, and an R2 bucket alone cannot make that check. We rejected the image CDN on cost. The quote came to $340/month at our current volume, against roughly $12/month for R2 storage plus Worker requests.

The Worker adds about 15ms per image request. We accepted those 15ms for per-request authorisation and a ~28x lower bill.

## Image storage: R2 served through a Worker

We store uploaded images in R2 and serve them through a Worker. We rejected two other options: a public R2 bucket on a custom domain, and a third-party image CDN.

Private galleries require an authorisation check on every request. A bucket alone does not supply that check, so we rejected the public bucket. The image CDN quote was $340/month at our current volume. R2 storage plus Worker requests costs roughly $12/month, so we rejected the image CDN on cost.

The Worker adds about 15ms per image request. We accepted that latency for per-request authorisation and a ~28x lower bill.

## Image storage and delivery

We store uploaded images in R2 and serve them through a Worker.

We rejected two alternatives. A public R2 bucket on a custom domain cannot serve
private galleries, because those require authorisation on each request. The
image CDN vendor quoted 340 dollars a month at the current volume; R2 storage
and Worker requests cost about 12 dollars a month.

The Worker adds about 15 milliseconds to each image request.

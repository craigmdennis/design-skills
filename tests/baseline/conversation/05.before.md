## Image storage: R2 behind a Worker

Uploaded images are stored in R2 and served through a Worker rather than from a public R2 bucket on a custom domain or a third-party image CDN.

The public bucket was ruled out because private galleries need an authorisation check on every request, which a bucket alone can't do. The image CDN was ruled out on cost: the quote came to $340/month at our current volume, against roughly $12/month for R2 storage plus Worker requests.

The tradeoff is latency — the Worker adds about 15ms per image request. We accepted that as the price of per-request authorisation and a ~28x lower bill.

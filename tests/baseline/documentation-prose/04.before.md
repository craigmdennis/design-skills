## 2.4.0 — 2026-08-01

### Changed

- The search index updates one document at a time instead of rebuilding. A full reindex takes about 3 seconds, down from about 90.

### Fixed

- Opening a document with no title crashed with a null reference error.

### Removed

- The `--verbose` flag. Use `--log-level debug` instead.

---

Two things I left out or would flag before you publish:

**The credit.** Aisha Nkemelu proposed the incremental index and Dan Rivera reviewed it on 2026-07-22. That's provenance, not a user-visible change, so it doesn't belong in the entry itself. If you credit contributors, the usual place is a trailing line — `Incremental index by Aisha Nkemelu, reviewed by Dan Rivera.` — with the review date dropped, since it tells a reader nothing they can act on.

**The version number.** Removing `--verbose` breaks any script that passes it. Under semver that's a major bump, not `2.4.0`. If the number is already shipped and fixed, the removal at least wants a louder marker than a bullet — a **Breaking** heading above it.

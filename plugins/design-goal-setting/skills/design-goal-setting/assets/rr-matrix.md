# Roles & responsibilities: behavioural matrix

A behavioural picture of the bar, used live in 1:1s. Observable behaviours, not
adjectives. For each behaviour, agree a frequency of evidence: how often you should be
able to point to a concrete example.

| Behaviour | What it looks like (observable) | Frequency of evidence |
|-----------|----------------------------------|------------------------|
| Defines "good" by impact and communication | Frames work by the business outcome and how clearly it was shared, not personal taste | <e.g. every initiative> |
| Designs from evidence | Decisions cite a user or data signal, not preference | <e.g. every major decision> |
| Works in the open | Posts a short walkthrough and a written update on a set cadence in a public channel | <e.g. weekly> |
| Makes decision-ready asks | Requests carry a clear ask, context, options with a recommendation, named owners, and a deadline | <e.g. each ask> |
| Manages up to decision-makers | Surfaces concerns early; frames updates for the people who decide | <e.g. monthly> |
| Owns measurable outcomes | Runs at least one initiative end to end with a baseline and a success measure | <e.g. each cycle> |

Adjust the behaviours to your level and discipline, and fill the frequency column with
what you and your manager agree.
